# Code of Conduct

SXSY-B122 welcomes good-faith technical criticism, corrections, experiments, and competing design conclusions.

Participants must:

- address the work rather than attack the person;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's identifying or confidential information;
- credit contributors and upstream projects accurately;
- disclose conflicts, commercial relationships, and copied material;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and sustained disruption; and
- follow maintainer directions intended to protect safety, privacy, attribution, or project scope.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these requirements. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's published contact channel.
